SIT223-SIT753 - Professional Practice in IT

Unit Overview

Content

To be successful IT graduates, students need to understand the use of industry tools and practices, how these tools work and connect together, and the underlying professional, ethical, and teamwork knowledge and skills required to put these into practice in a professional manner.

Hurdle Requirement
