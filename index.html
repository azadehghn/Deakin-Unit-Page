SIT223-SIT753 - Professional Practice in IT

Unit details

Year: 2023 unit information
Enrolment modes: Trimester 1: Burwood (Melbourne), Geelong, Cloud (online)
Credit point(s): 1
Unit Chair: Trimester 1: Azadeh Ghari Neiat

Unit Content

SIT223-SIT753 Professional Practice in IT provides students with professional practice knowledge and skills required for working in the information technology industry.

The unit focuses on professional practice, teamwork, communication, project management and ethical responsibilities in IT.
