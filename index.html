SIT223-SIT753 - Professional Practice in IT

Content

Unit Overview

To be successful IT graduates need to understand the use of industry tools and practices, the ways these tools work and connect together, and the underlying professional, ethical, and teamwork knowledge and skills needed to put these into practice in a professional manner.

Key Topics

  • Agile project management and collaborative workflows
  • Version control and continuous delivery pipelines
  • IT ethics, governance, and professional codes of conduct
  • Emerging technologies and industry diversity

Professional Practice

Students develop practical experience by applying professional standards, collaborative teamwork strategies, and ethical principles to real-world IT projects.

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply industry-standard tools, version control, and agile methodologies to IT projects.
  • Demonstrate ethical decision-making and adherence to professional IT codes of conduct.
  • Collaborate effectively within diverse teams using professional communication strategies.
  • Critically reflect on professional identity, career readiness, and emerging trends in the IT sector.

Assessment

Learning Portfolio: 100%
