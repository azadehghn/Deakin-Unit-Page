Unit Content

SIT223-SIT753 Professional Practice in IT develops students' professional skills and knowledge required for working effectively in the information technology industry. The unit covers professional practice, teamwork, communication, software development processes, version control, cybersecurity awareness, ethical responsibilities and working collaboratively on IT projects.

Students develop practical skills through teamwork and project-based activities while learning how professional IT teams plan, develop, manage and deliver software solutions.

Content

To be successful IT graduates need to understand the use of industry tools and practices, the ways these tools work and connect together, and the underlying professional, ethical, and teamwork knowledge and skills needed to put these into practice in a professional manner.
