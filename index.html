SIT223-SIT753 - Professional Practice in IT

Content

To succeed, IT graduates must develop an understanding of industry tools and practices, how these tools function and integrate with one another, as well as the professional, ethical, and teamwork skills required to apply them effectively in a professional context. They should also be able to adapt to rapidly evolving technologies, apply problem-solving and critical thinking in real-world situations, and communicate their ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. Furthermore, graduates need to demonstrate accountability, collaboration, and continuous learning to thrive in diverse and dynamic workplace environments.
