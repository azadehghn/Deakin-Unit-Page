Unit Content

SIT223-SIT753 Professional Practice in IT provides students with professional practice knowledge and skills required for working in the information technology industry.

The unit focuses on professional practice, teamwork, communication, project management and ethical responsibilities in IT.

Content

To be successful IT graduates need to understand the use of industry tools and practices, the ways these tools work and connect together, and the underlying professional, ethical, and teamwork knowledge and skills needed to put these into practice in a professional manner.
